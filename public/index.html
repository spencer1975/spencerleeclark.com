Obsidian plugin · v1.0

Midjourney Viewer

Browse your downloaded Midjourney images inside Obsidian with the full prompt for every image, read from the metadata Midjourney embeds rather than the truncated filename. Search, a lightbox, and galleries you can drop into any note. Entirely offline.

How it works

Three steps to your gallery

01
Install from ObsidianSettings → Community plugins → Browse → “Midjourney Viewer”. One click, no account.
02
Point it at your downloadsPick the folder where Midjourney saves your images. Thumbnails and prompts are indexed once and cached.
03
Search, browse, embedPress / to search like Midjourney: "--ar 16:9" city -neon. Drop galleries into any note with a dialog, no syntax.
